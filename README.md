📊 Retail Inventory & Demand Analytics

📌 Project Overview

The Retail Inventory & Demand Analytics project focuses on analyzing retail sales, inventory levels, product demand, and revenue using Microsoft Excel.

The goal of this project is to transform raw retail data into meaningful business insights through data cleaning, data analysis, PivotTables, PivotCharts, and an interactive Excel dashboard.

🎯 Project Objectives

Analyze overall retail sales and revenue performance.

Identify top-performing product categories and products.

Understand monthly revenue trends.

Monitor inventory levels and identify potential stock risks.

Build an interactive dashboard for business decision-making.

Present key findings through data visualization.

🧹 Data Cleaning & Preparation

The following data preparation activities were performed:

Explored the dataset structure and column information.

Checked for missing values.

Investigated duplicate records.

Reviewed data types and date formatting.

Organized the raw and cleaned datasets.

Created additional calculated columns for analysis.

📈 Analysis Performed

1. Category-wise Revenue Analysis

Analyzed revenue generated by different product categories to understand category-level performance.

2. Top-Selling Products Analysis

Identified products with the highest total units sold.

3. Monthly Revenue Analysis

Analyzed revenue trends over time to understand monthly business performance.

4. Inventory Stock Status Analysis

Classified inventory into three categories:

Out of Stock

Potential Stock Risk

Sufficient Stock

5. Revenue Calculations

Created calculated columns using Excel formulas:

Gross Sales = Units Sold × Price

Discount Amount = Gross Sales × Discount Rate

Net Revenue = Gross Sales − Discount Amount

Note: Actual profit was not calculated because product purchase cost data was not available.

🏁 Conclusion

This project demonstrates how Microsoft Excel can be used to perform retail data analysis and create an interactive business dashboard.

By combining data cleaning, calculated columns, PivotTables, PivotCharts, and data visualization, the project converts raw retail data into meaningful insights that can support better inventory monitoring and business decisions.
